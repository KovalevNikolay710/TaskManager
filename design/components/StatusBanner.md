# StatusBanner
"Выполнена" banner on the task screen: bg `--color-success-soft`, `--radius-lg`, icon `check-circle` `--color-success`, title semibold, caption `--text-xs` muted ("Отмечена сегодня, 11:05" from `UpdatedAt`), right: secondary button "Вернуть в работу" (icon `undo`); on narrow screens the button wraps under the text. `role="status"`.
For an active task, in its place: full-width secondary button "✓ Отметить выполненной" (icon `check` `--color-success`).
