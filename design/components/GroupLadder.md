# GroupLadder (groups screen)
Ladder of 10 steps: ×10 at top, ×1 at bottom. Groups stand on steps; several groups may share a step. Insertion rule: [ladder-insert](../rules/ladder-insert.md).
- Container `ladder` (`<ol aria-label="Лесенка групп: сверху самые важные">`): card `--color-surface`, `--radius-lg`, `--shadow-sm`, padding `--space-2` 0. Modifiers `ladder--dragging`, `ladder--placing`. Each step is a list `aria-label="Ступень ×N"`.
- **Step** (`ladder__step .w-N`, `style="--n: N"`): two columns. "Handrail" (76px; desktop 112px): coloured bar 10px high whose width grows with weight (`6px + N*3.4px`, desktop `10px + N*6px`) and "×N" (`--text-xs` semibold). Right: group cards (wrap, gap `--space-2`).
  - Occupied (`--busy`): height >= 56px, bar full colour, "×N" `--w-text`.
  - Empty: height 36px, bar opacity 0.35, "×N" muted; caption "свободно" visible only while moving a group.
  - All 10 steps always visible so layout does not jump at drag start.
- **LadderGroup** (`ladder-group .w-N`): height >= 44px, `--radius-md`, bg `--w-soft`, left bar 3px `--w`.
  - Main part: button "Изменить" (opens [Sheet](Sheet.md) with [GroupForm](GroupForm.md)): name (`--text-sm` medium, ellipsis after ~22 chars) and meta "4 задачи · 1 выполнена" / "3 задачи" / "Нет задач" (`--text-xs`, `--w-text`); weight > 10 (legacy) sits on ×10 with "×12" in meta. `aria-label="Учёба, вес 3, 4 задачи, 1 выполнена. Изменить"`.
  - Right: handle ⋮⋮ (`ladder-group__handle`, icon `grip`, 36px with 44px target, `touch-action: none`, cursor `grab`): drag; short tap/Enter = placement mode. `aria-label="Переместить «Учёба» на другую ступень"`.
  - States: `--lifted` (dragging: `--shadow-lg`, rotate -1.5deg, scale 1.03), `--ghost` (old place: opacity 0.35, dashed `--w`), `--moving` (placement mode: 2px `--color-focus`), `--pending` (saving: opacity 0.6; handles of all groups disabled until response), shift preview "→ ×4" in meta (`ladder-group__shift`, semibold).
  - "Без группы" (`--static`): not a button, no handle, bg `--color-surface-sunken`, dashed `--color-border-strong`, always on ×1: "Без группы · N задач · всегда ×1" (shown whenever >= 1 group exists, even with 0 tasks).
  - Groups within a step: alphabetical, case-insensitive.
- **Drag** (pointer): starts on the handle after > 6px move (mouse: at once; no long press). Target under pointer: over a step row -> step bg `--color-accent-soft` (`ladder__step--hot`); within +-10px of the border of two **occupied** steps -> insertion line (`ladder__gap`: 4px `--color-accent` with ring `--color-accent-soft` and plate "Вставить на ×3", `--color-accent` bg, `--color-text-inverse` text). Groups that will shift show "→ ×4" live. No free step: line not highlighted, hint "Нет свободной ступени — поставьте рядом". Release -> move; release outside / Esc -> cancel (card returns, `--duration-normal`). Autoscroll at screen edge; page scroll disabled only via `touch-action: none` on the handle.
- **Placement mode** (keyboard/screen reader/tap): tap/Enter/Space on the handle -> [PlaceBanner](PlaceBanner.md) above; every step except the current one gets a button `ladder__place` ("Поставить на ×N" on empty, "Сюда, рядом" on occupied; 36px, 44px target, dashed 1.5px `--w`, text `--w-text`); between two occupied steps a full-width button `ladder__gap-btn` (44px, `--text-xs` `--color-accent`) showing consequences: "Между ×2 и ×3: встанет на ×3, поднимутся: Учёба ×4, Английский ×5"; no free step -> disabled "нет свободной ступени". Moved group outlined `--color-focus`. Focus goes to the first target; Tab/Shift+Tab and up/down move between targets (roving tabindex), Enter selects, Esc cancels (focus back to the handle). Selecting performs the move and leaves the mode.
- Result is announced via [Toast](Toast.md) (`role="status"`). Colour is always duplicated by "×N".
